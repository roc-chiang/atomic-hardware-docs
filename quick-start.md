# Quick-Start Guide: Brand Personalities & HUD Control

Welcome to **Atomic Forge Commerce**, a premium multi-industry e-commerce framework designed to support tactical hardware, industrial CNC products, reference-grade audio equipment, rugged outdoor gear, and luxury lifestyle tools.

This guide details how to configure the **Preset System** and **HUD Aesthetic Detailing Level Control**.

---

## 1. Selecting a Brand Personality

Atomic Forge supports 5 dynamic brand presets. When you select a personality, the theme automatically updates accent colors, call-to-actions (CTAs), badge formats, and copywriting vocabulary.

### How to Configure:
1. From your Shopify admin, go to **Online Store** > **Themes**.
2. Click **Customize** next to the active theme.
3. Click the gear icon (**Theme settings**) in the sidebar, and select **Colors & Materials**.
4. Locate the **Brand Personality (Presets)** dropdown and select one of the following:
   - **Atomic (EDC / Tactical / Titanium)**: Bright tritium green highlights (`#9CFF00`), military nomenclature, high-intensity telemetry indicators.
   - **Industrial (Smart Hardware / CNC)**: Ice blue highlights (`#5EDBFF`), engineering specifications, clean structural tags.
   - **Audio (DAC / Audio Hardware)**: Laser orange highlights (`#FF6B00`), frequency-response curves, acoustic specifications.
   - **Outdoor (Camping / Wilderness)**: Rugged yellow-green highlights (`#E8FF7A`), wilderness survival tags, environmental ratings.
   - **Premium (Coffee / Luxury Tools)**: Warm ivory gray highlights (`#D8D3C5`), sleek minimal layout, clean artisanal titles.

---

## 2. Adjusting the HUD Aesthetic Level

To prevent "Cyberpunk fatigue" and align layouts with cleaner, premium industries, the theme provides three intensity configurations for technical instrumentation overlays.

### How to Configure:
1. In **Theme settings** > **Colors & Materials**, find **HUD Detailing Level**.
2. Select one of the three levels:
   - **Minimal**: Disables CRT scanlines, background blueprint grids, laser LQIP scanlines, and diagnostic tags. Recommended for Premium/Luxury brands.
   - **Standard**: Tones down grid and scanline overlays to 70% opacity, providing subtle structural framing. Recommended for Audio and Industrial hardware.
   - **Tactical**: Enables full 100% telemetry detail, CRT screen scanlines, pulsing scanner preloaders, and diagnostic code tags. Recommended for Tactical/EDC gear.

## 3. Communication Frequencies

The localization selector maps language targets to simulated tuner frequencies:
* **English (EN)**: `88.1 MHz` (Tactical Baseline)
* **German (DE)**: `92.3 MHz` (Industrial Hub)
* **Japanese (JA)**: `98.5 MHz` (Audiophile Reference)
* **Outdoor Baseline**: `101.1 MHz` (Expedition Signal)
* **Chinese (ZH)**: `104.7 MHz` (Premium Standard)
