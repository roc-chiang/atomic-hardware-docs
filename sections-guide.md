# Custom Section Marketplace Manual

**Atomic Forge Commerce** provides a library of 17 modular sections. Below is a detailed review of the 7 new high-fidelity modules introduced in the Section Marketplace Expansion.

---

## 1. Comparison Pro Matrix (`sections/comparison-pro.liquid`)
- **Purpose**: A side-by-side spec checklist comparing your custom product to standard off-the-shelf alternatives.
- **Controls & Settings**:
  - Configure titles, description text blocks, and sidebar headers.
  - Add feature rows (blocks) with customizable icons (checkmark or cross), our specifications, and competitor specifications.

---

## 2. Timeline Pro Roadmap (`sections/timeline-pro.liquid`)
- **Purpose**: A technical roadmap showcasing design stages, engineering phases, verification dates, and laboratory logs.
- **Controls & Settings**:
  - Add milestones containing dates, status node styles (pulsing active, solid completed, upcoming), titles, descriptions, and audit logs.

---

## 3. Patent IP Showcase (`sections/patent-showcase.liquid`)
- **Purpose**: Displays industrial design patents, utility filings, and CNC blueprints to establish engineering credibility.
- **Controls & Settings**:
  - Interactive blueprint layout showcasing caliper annotations and dimension guidelines.
  - Blocks to list patent codes (e.g., US-11942-B2), approval statuses, and structural details.

---

## 4. Material Property Explorer (`sections/material-explorer.liquid`)
- **Purpose**: An interactive metallurgical laboratory console powered by Alpine.js. Allows users to compare alloys.
- **Controls & Settings**:
  - Preloaded with data for Grade 5 Titanium, 6061-T6 Aluminum, Carbon Fiber, and Stainless Steel.
  - Dynamically updates progress bars indicating tensile strength, weight density, corrosion index, and heat dispersion.

---

## 5. Team Members Directory (`sections/team-members.liquid`)
- **Purpose**: Highlights machine shop programmers, design directors, and verification auditors to humanize precision manufacturing.
- **Controls & Settings**:
  - Add operator blocks containing operator serial IDs, division badges, names, roles, station codes, and biography summaries.

---

## 6. Compliance & Certificates (`sections/certifications.liquid`)
- **Purpose**: A compliance grid displaying quality certifications such as CE, FCC, RoHS, and MIL-STD-810H.
- **Controls & Settings**:
  - Custom grid of verification badges containing certificate codes, conformance names, test details, and certificate IDs.

---

## 7. Authorized Stockists (`sections/retail-partners.liquid`)
- **Purpose**: A dealer hub mapping physical stockists and flagship stores carrying your batches.
- **Controls & Settings**:
  - Blocks to input cities, GPS coordinates, store names, stockist tiers, addresses, and inventory status indicators.
